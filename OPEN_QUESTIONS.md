# Client decisions before launch

1. **Booking policy:** whole-day or separate slots? Confirm opening hours, venue availability, capacities, blackout dates and whether instant reservations also publish events immediately.
2. **Official identity:** confirm the contact email, organising committee name and the three-level partner hierarchy. Supply approved UfM/ALF logos and explicitly decide whether the Ministry of Foreign Affairs appears.
3. **Approved content:** supply photography/permissions, final programme and news, checked Arabic/English copy, exact venue pins and verified accessibility information. All current events and news are samples.
4. **Submission and privacy workflow:** how will bilingual submissions, expected attendance and attachments be stored and reviewed? Confirm the data controller, contact channel, retention period and consent wording for the production service.
5. **Launch ownership:** confirm the production domain and deployment owner, and provide the Mapbox token/published Studio style. Identify who will connect the future backend, secure administration and operate scheduled publishing.
